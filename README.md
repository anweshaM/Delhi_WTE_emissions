# Delhi_WTE_emissions
This repository contains the calculations and other information regarding the climate impact of waste-to-energy plants in Delhi, India.

Only "plastic" waste is considered. The data for processed plastic waste the four Delhi WTE plants in 2023 was obtained from the Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) portal of the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) reported by the Centre for Science and Environment, India (https://www.cseindia.org/epr-portal-insights-a-deep-dive-into-india-s-centralized-portal-for-plastic-packaging-12443). 

We applied equation 5.1 of IPCC Guidelines for National Greenhouse Gas Inventories (https://www.ipcc-nggip.iges.or.jp/public/2006gl/pdf/5_Volume5/V5_5_Ch5_IOB.pdf).
